# Code of Conduct

Participation in Phylomatic spaces should be professional, constructive, and
focused on improving the software and documentation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when discussing bugs, scientific assumptions, and design tradeoffs.
- Keep reports reproducible and specific.
- Credit related tools, datasets, and prior work.
- Avoid sharing private clinical, student, or unpublished sequence data in
  public channels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Publishing private data without permission
- Personal attacks instead of technical critique
- Spam or off-topic promotion

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close comments and issues that violate this code
of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in blocked participation
in project spaces.
